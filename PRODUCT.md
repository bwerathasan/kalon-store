# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Two audiences, both consuming via mobile, RTL Arabic:
- **Cold ad/influencer traffic** landing on `ar/rouge.html` or `ar/duo.html` — skeptical about whether
  this is a real seller or a disappearing Instagram perfume scam, deciding fast, low deliberation.
- **Direct/organic visitors** landing on `ar/index.html` (the storefront) — browsing, comparing the
  two products, more considered but still expect a fast, frictionless COD purchase path.
Market: Arab-48 (Palestinian citizens of Israel) towns — Haifa, Sakhnin, Kafr Qara, Acre, Nazareth,
Tayibe, Umm al-Fahm, Tamra, Shefa-Amr. Cash-on-delivery is the dominant, trust-critical payment mode.

## Product Purpose

SILLAGE is a D2C fragrance brand selling two products: a single Extrait de Parfum (ROUGE, 250₪) and
a gift duo (Citruc & Sweet, 300₪, genuinely limited to 40 sets). Success = fast, trustworthy
conversions from ad/influencer traffic and browsers alike, without ever fabricating trust signals
(fake stock counts, fake live-viewer numbers) — this was explicitly rejected once already this
project when a competitor's fake-scarcity pattern was copied too literally.

## Brand Personality

Premium but reachable, warm, honest. Three words: **trustworthy, warm, confident** — not cold-luxury,
not discount-bin. Gold as an earned accent (matches the actual bottle cap), not a blanket skin.

## Anti-references

- **jo.topperfumes.net** — useful for structural/CRO patterns (risk-reversal bar, gallery+price+CTA
  ordering, FAQ, review format) but its literal visual skin (navy background, red circular discount
  badges, emoji-based trust icons, fake "58 people viewing" / "9 left in stock" counters) must NOT be
  copied. That skin reads as a generic dropship/dupe site; SILLAGE's own gold/black/cream identity
  and honest-only urgency is the deliberate departure from it.
- Fake urgency of any kind (invented stock numbers, invented viewer counts) — hard reject.
- Generic "AI landing page" scaffolding: gradient text, tiny uppercase tracked eyebrows on every
  section, numbered 01/02/03 section markers, identical icon-card grids — avoid by default per this
  skill's own house rules, doubly so since the brand's design already has an established personality.

## Design Principles

1. **Real over dramatic.** Every urgency/scarcity claim must be backed by a real number (Duo's 40-set
   cap is real and can be used aggressively; ROUGE's 130-unit stock is not scarce and must not claim
   to be — use the honest daily price-reset countdown there instead).
2. **Gold is earned, not default.** Reserve gold for CTAs, price, and accents that tie back to the
   product itself (the bottle cap). Don't let it become wallpaper.
3. **Landing pages have no exits.** `rouge.html` and `duo.html` are for cold ad traffic — no site nav,
   every element drives toward the one CTA. The storefront (`index.html`) is the one page that
   should have real navigation.
4. **Trust is built with specifics, not adjectives.** Reviews are grounded in real Arab-48 gifting
   scenarios (engagement gifts, wedding "شبكة" gifts, fear of counterfeits) with real town names, not
   generic 5-star filler.
5. **Structure can borrow, skin cannot.** When referencing competitor sites for inspiration, adopt
   proven information architecture, never their literal color system or fabricated trust mechanics.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

RTL Arabic throughout (`dir="rtl"`, Arabic-first typography: Cormorant Garamond for accents,
Noto Serif Arabic for headlines, IBM Plex Sans Arabic for body). No stated WCAG target beyond
standard contrast/legibility — mobile-first, since traffic is overwhelmingly mobile.
